ynoTV includes a high-performance, native Rust engine specifically optimized for synchronizing and browsing massive VOD (Video on Demand) libraries.

## Browsing Movies & Series

Access your VOD content directly from the sidebar or via the dedicated **Movies** and **Series** views.

### Features
- **Smart Metadata**: Automatic integration with TMDB for posters, ratings, and plot summaries.
- **Search & Discovery**: Instantly find any title across all your connected sources.
- **SubSource Integration**: Retrieve missing subtitles directly from SubSource for seamless playback within the player.

<Aside type="tip">
  Configure your TMDB API key in **Settings > TMDB** to enable rich metadata and high-quality posters for your VOD library.
</Aside>

### Sorting

Every VOD view offers a **Sort** dropdown with ascending/descending order for each option:

- **A-Z** — alphabetical by title.
- **Recently Added** — newest syncs first.
- **Year** — by release year.
- **Rating** — by score.
- **Last Watched** — by your viewing history, most recent first.

## Category Management

Streamline your VOD browsing experience by organizing which content categories appear in your Movies and Series views.

**Access Method**
:   Right-click any source in the **Movies** or **Series** view and select **Manage VOD Categories**

**Hide Categories**
:   Selectively disable categories to exclude them from your browsing interface. Hidden categories will not display content in category views and their items will be excluded from search results, providing a cleaner, more focused content discovery experience.

## Playlists

Create **VOD playlists** to organize movies and TV episodes across sources into your own collections:

- **Create Playlist** — name a new playlist, then **Add to Playlist** from any movie/series (or **Add Selected** in bulk).
- **Randomize List** — shuffle the order on demand.
- **Autoplay next on playlist** — keep playing through the playlist without interruption.
- **Rename / Delete / Remove from playlist** — manage your playlists at any time.
- Items from a deleted or disabled source are kept but shown as **hidden** in the playlist until the source returns.

## Trailers

Movies and series can play a **trailer** with a single click. Trailer options (available from the title's player menu) control where it comes from:

- **Use trailer from the IPTV source** — the provider's own trailer stream, if one exists.
- **Use trailer from TMDB** — the official TMDB trailer, played via YouTube (powered by yt-dlp).

The trailer plays in your chosen player mode (embedded, popout, or external). If neither source has a trailer, the app tells you **"No trailer available"** for that title.

<Aside type="tip">
  If YouTube trailers fail with 403 errors, update yt-dlp from **Settings → About → Updates** — YouTube frequently breaks older versions.
</Aside>

## Series Playback

### Autoplay Next Episode

Under **Settings → Playback → VOD Settings**, **Auto-Play Next Episode** automatically plays the next episode of a series when the current one ends — and if it's the end of a season, it starts the first episode of the next season automatically.

### Queueing Episodes

Series can be **queued** for continuous playback — queue a single season (**Queueing Season...**) or the entire show (**Queueing all seasons...**), with progress shown as episodes are queued.

## Progress Tracking

Maintain your viewing history automatically with the Recently Watched feature, which tracks playback progress across all movies and series.

**Tracking Mechanism**
:   Progress is automatically saved to the **Recently Watched** list on both the Home page and the Recent tab

**Update Frequency**
:   - Automatic updates every 30 seconds during active playback
   - Immediate save when pressing **Stop** or switching to Live TV

**Progress Threshold**
:   Watch progress is only recorded when viewing is between **5% and 95%** completion. This prevents saving entries for briefly sampled content while ensuring significant viewing sessions are tracked

VOD and Series browsing is currently supported for:
- **Xtream Codes** (Native API support)
- **Stalker Portals**
- **Local M3U Playlists** (Partial support via groups)
