ynoTV can be driven entirely from a **game controller**, an **HTPC wireless keyboard remote**, or your **phone** — no mouse needed. Everything lives in **Settings → Controllers & Remote**, and all controller features are driven by a single pipeline: a raw button press is mapped through your custom button mappings and chord combos, then executed as a spatial-navigation action across the UI.

## Spatial Navigation

When controller navigation is enabled, ynoTV switches to **spatial navigation**: instead of clicking with a mouse, you move a highlight between on-screen elements with the D-Pad and confirm with buttons.

- **D-Pad / left stick** — move the highlight between elements (channels, menu items, grid tiles).
- **A (South)** — select / confirm the highlighted element.
- **B (East)** — back / exit the current view.
- **Holding a D-Pad direction** repeats the press with an accelerating rate, so holding down will keep moving through lists (the repeat delay and speed are adjustable).

The **live monitor** panel shows detected gamepads, connection state, and a live button readout as you press them, so you can verify everything is working before navigating.

## Button Mapping

Every physical button on your controller can be remapped. Open **Settings → Controllers & Remote → Configure Gamepad**, then press a button to assign it to any action — the app captures your physical button and binds it to the action you choose.

- Remaps apply **live** on boot and on settings import, and are saved as a **custom profile**.
- There's a live visualizer that shows your controller graphic lighting up as you press buttons, so you can confirm mappings at a glance.
- **Reset to Defaults** restores the factory mapping at any time.

## Button Combinations (Chords)

Holding a **modifier** button and pressing another button triggers an extra shortcut — the classic "chord" pattern from console remotes. The modifiers are **L1, R1, L2, R2**.

For example, you could hold **L1** and press **A** to jump straight to Live TV, or hold **R2** and press **Up** to page up the EPG. Open **Button Combinations** to see the full grid and assign each chord to a shortcut action.

## Keyboard as Controller

If you're using an HTPC setup with a wireless keyboard remote (like a Rii-style air mouse), you can bind **physical keyboard keys to pretend to be controller buttons**. The keyboard then drives the *exact same* pipeline as a real gamepad — your button remaps, chord combos, spatial navigation, hold-repeat, and the on-screen visualizer all work identically.

### Getting started

1. Go to **Settings → Controllers & Remote → Keyboard as Controller** and enable it.
2. **Arrow keys** (D-Pad), **Enter** (A), and **Escape** (B) are pre-bound so it works out of the box.
3. Click any button card and press the key you want to assign — Ctrl, Shift, and Alt are supported as distinct keys and map cleanly (either side of the key triggers the binding).

### How it behaves

- **Mapped keys take over** — they act as controller buttons and won't double-fire the normal keyboard shortcuts. **Unmapped keys keep their normal behavior.**
- **Typing always wins** — while focused in a search box, letters and editing keys (Enter, Backspace) keep working normally for typing, while the D-Pad and Escape still act as controller buttons so you can move away from the field.
- **Bind a modifier to a chord** — map e.g. Ctrl to L1 and the chord system works from the keyboard too (Ctrl + Enter triggers whatever you assigned to L1 + A).

<Aside type="tip">
Since the keys are mapped to *buttons*, the on-screen keyboard is optional — just select a text field with the D-Pad and type directly on the physical keyboard.
</Aside>

## Phone Remote

Turn any smartphone into a wireless TV remote. **Settings → Controllers & Remote → Phone Remote** (or the phone icon) starts a local server on your PC, and your phone pairs over your local Wi-Fi.

### Pairing

1. Make sure your phone is on the **same Wi-Fi network** as the PC.
2. Enable the phone remote server — it's **off by default** and only starts when you turn it on.
3. **Scan the QR code** with your phone's camera, or open the connection URL shown in the settings page.

Once connected you get a full wireless touchpad, D-Pad, volume and channel rockers, and shortcut buttons.

<Aside type="tip">
**Add the remote to your home screen** for one-tap access instead of typing the URL every time.

**On Android** (Chrome, Samsung Internet, etc.):
1. Open the remote URL in your mobile browser.
2. Tap the **three dots** menu in the top-right corner.
3. Select **Add to Home screen** (or **Install app**).
4. Tap **Install** or **Add** to confirm.

**On iPhone (iOS Safari):**
1. Open the remote URL in Safari.
2. Tap the **Share** button at the bottom of the screen (the square with an arrow pointing up).
3. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**.

A shortcut to the remote will appear on your home screen — tap it and the remote opens straight to ynoTV.
</Aside>

### Navigation Tabs

The bottom bar of the remote has tabs you can show, hide, and reorder — and you can pick which one is the **default landing tab**. Available tabs include:

- **Guide** — browse categories and the full channel list, tuned to match the desktop guide's ordering, names, favorites, and custom categories. Selecting a channel tunes it on the desktop, and the currently playing channel is highlighted.
- **Sports** — live scores for your followed leagues, with game links that tune the matching broadcast on the desktop.
- **Multiview** — pick the multiview layout and engine (HLS or MPV), and watch the multiview slots update live.
- **Touchpad / remote** — the classic touchpad, D-Pad, and media controls.

### Customizing the remote

The **Phone Remote Customizer** in Settings lets you personalize what your phone sees:

- **Skins & themes** — pick a visual skin; updates instantly on connected phones.
- **Corner satellite buttons** — configure the 4 shortcut buttons around the D-Pad dial.
- **Middle action buttons** — remap the Back and Play/Pause buttons between the rockers and adjust their size (Compact / Normal / Large / Expanded).
- **Quick action buttons** — add, remove, and reorder the quick buttons below the D-Pad.
- **Display options** — show or hide the Now Playing banner, search bar, volume rocker, and channel rocker.

There's a **live interactive preview** in the customizer so you can see changes before they hit your phone.

<Aside type="note">
The phone remote respects your desktop settings — including failover behavior (Always Play Primary / Keep View on Selected Channel), manual channel ordering, edited channel names, category filters, and the multiview engine selection.
</Aside>
