Casting to any **Chromecast-compatible device** is now supported, allowing you to stream live TV, movies, and series directly to your TV or display.

## Enabling Google Cast

To get started, enable Google Cast under **Settings → Playback → Google Cast**.

<Aside type="note">
A Windows firewall prompt will appear on first use to allow local network scanning for available devices. Accept the prompt to discover and connect to Chromecast devices on your network.
</Aside>

## How It Works

Once enabled, a cast button will appear in the playback controls. Tap it to select your device and start streaming wirelessly.
