**Widgets** put extra information and quick navigation right on your screen — either as overlays on the playback view or as boxes on the home screen. Widgets are fully customizable: scale, background opacity, and which ones appear are all under your control.

## Adding Widgets

- **On the playback screen** — right-click the background or stream → **Add Widget**, then pick from the available widget types.
- **On the home screen** — use the **Widgets menu** (it auto-hides when a stream is playing; right-click the home screen to reopen it during Live TV playback).
- The **Active Widgets** panel shows what's currently enabled, with one-click removal, and an **Add Widget** section for adding more.

## Widget Types

### Live Sports Scores

Displays live scores for ongoing matches along the top of the overlay. Click a score to view detailed match information, or right-click to hide specific matches. Available in two modes:

- **Persistent** — remains visible when the UI overlay auto-hides.
- **Autohide** — hides alongside the UI overlay.

The Sports widget must be enabled (via the right-click context menu) before its overlay settings apply.

### Live Games Sidebar

The sports **Live Games Sidebar** — a tab on the right edge of the main screen listing every live game with a count, scores, and one-tap access to streams. Can be removed from the Active Widgets panel. See the [Sports guide](./sports-hub/) for details.

### Recent Channels

Shows recently watched channels along with their currently airing programme. Click any entry to switch to that channel. The number of entries is configurable (e.g. **Recent 5** or 10).

### Favorites

Displays your favorite channels with their current programmes for one-click switching — a favorites rail right on the overlay.

### Custom Group Widget

Displays channels and their currently airing programmes from a selected **custom group**, enabling quick channel switching directly from the overlay.

### What's Next Widget

Shows the next scheduled programme on the current channel — handy for deciding whether to stay tuned or switch.

## Customizing Widgets

Under **Settings → Live TV → Widgets & Scales**:

- **Widget Scale** — scales the entire widget box and text (default 100%), with quick presets.
- **Background Opacity** — how transparent the widget box background is; lower values are more see-through.
- **Live preview** — see the current scale and opacity applied to a sample widget as you adjust.
- **Sports Scores Overlay** — separate controls for the scores bar: **Overlay Scale** (height of the bar) and its gradient **background opacity**.

<Aside type="tip">
Widgets scale with your UI — if you change the application UI scale, widget sizing follows along, so a scale preset of 100% is the right baseline for most setups.
</Aside>
