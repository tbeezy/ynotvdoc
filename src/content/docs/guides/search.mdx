ynoTV's search engine is designed for speed, allowing you to filter through thousands of channels and Electronic Program Guide (EPG) entries in milliseconds.

## Search Mechanics

### Quick Access
Press the default search key **(S)** to instantly focus the search input in the title bar.

### Scope of Search
ynoTV indexes the following data for real-time results:
- **Channel Names**: Matches against all active provider channel lists.
- **EPG Data**: Scans current and upcoming program titles and descriptions.

### Visibility Filters
Your global search results respect your privacy and organization settings. Any sources, categories, or individual channels that you have **disabled** in Settings will not appear in search results.

## Results Tabs

Search results are organized into **three tabs**:

| Tab | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Channels** | Matching channels, displayed in the same style as the Live TV channel list |
| **Live Now EPG** | Currently airing programs that match your query, with **time remaining**, start/end times, and a **progress bar** showing how far the program is through its slot |
| **Upcoming EPG** | Future programs matching your query, with their start and end times |

EPG tabs only appear when your query matches program data — a pure channel query shows just the Channels tab.

## Advanced Search

Click the **search icon** at the end of the search bar to open **Advanced Search**, giving you precise control over what gets searched:

- **Search Term** — your query (at least 2 characters).
- **Search In** — **Channels**, **EPG Only**, or **Both**.
- **Sources & Categories** — restrict the search to specific sources and/or categories instead of everything enabled.
- **Use these settings for regular title bar searches** — apply the same scope to the regular search bar, so you don't have to open Advanced Search every time.

<Aside type="tip">
Search is case-insensitive and supports partial matching, allowing you to find "BBC One" simply by typing "bbc". Multi-word queries are matched intelligently, and full support for Cyrillic and other non-Latin scripts (including accent folding and transliteration) means "perviy" matches "Первый".
</Aside>

## Search Results Limit

Customize the maximum number of search results displayed to match your preferences:

**Configuration Path**
:   Navigate to **Settings → Channels → Max search results**

**Default Value**
:   The system is pre-configured with a limit of **1000 results**.

**Adjustment Options**
:   Increase or decrease this threshold based on your source volume and browsing preferences (capped at 5000). Results are **virtualized**, so even large values stay smooth. The same limit applies to channel search, custom group search, and the calendar channel selector.
