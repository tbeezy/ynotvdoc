The **Popout Player** allows you to stream channels in a dedicated popout MPV window, providing a separate viewing experience that can be moved, resized, or placed on a second monitor.

## Playing in Popout

Right-click any channel and select **Play in Popout** to open it in a separate MPV window.

## Always on Top

The popout window can be configured to stay on top of other windows. Navigate to **Settings → Popout Player** and enable the **Always on Top** option.

## Popout Mode for Live TV EPG

Activate **Popout Mode** via the icon on the middle-right of the Live TV EPG. When enabled, all channel selections will be routed directly to the popout player instead of playing in the main interface.

## External Player Support

Any channel can be sent to an external player of your choice. Configure the player under **Settings → Playback → External/Popout Player**.

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Right-Click Access" icon="menu">
    Right-click any channel and select **Send to External Player** to open it in your configured external player.
  </Card>
  <Card title="EPG Routing" icon="tv">
    Click the screen icon in the EPG and set it to **External** to route all playback to your external player by default.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

<Aside type="tip">
External player support gives you the flexibility to use advanced media players like VLC, MPC-HC, or any other player you prefer for playback.
</Aside>
