The Sports Hub is a dedicated dashboard for sports enthusiasts, aggregating live scores, upcoming schedules, and league-specific data from ESPN.

## Viewing Features

### Live Games Search

The Sports Hub provides integrated search functionality on match cards in both the **Live Now** and **Upcoming** tabs, enabling quick access to available broadcast sources:

**Search Teams**
:   Opens the search interface with both team names pre-populated, allowing you to browse all available channels and manually select your preferred broadcast.

**Show Search Results**
:   Displays matched channels directly on the match card, enabling one-click access to the broadcast without navigating away from the Sports Hub.

This streamlined workflow ensures you can locate and launch live sports coverage efficiently, whether you prefer browsing all available options or selecting from curated results.

### In-Page Streaming
Click the **Stream Icon** next to the Sports Hub header to pin your current live stream directly onto the sports dashboard. This allows you to track scores across multiple leagues while never losing sight of the action—similar to the EPG view.

## Personalized Experience

### Favorite Teams
You can follow specific teams to have them appear prominently in the **Teams** tab:
1. Navigate to the **Leagues** section.
2. Select your league and find your team.
3. Mark them as a favorite to track their progress throughout the season.

### Hub Settings
Access the **Settings** gear within the Sports Hub to customize your view:
- **Display Preferences**: Toggle which leagues and stats are shown.
- **Auto-Update**: The "Live Now" page automatically refreshes every 30 seconds during active games to ensure scores are always current.
